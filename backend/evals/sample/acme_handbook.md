# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook (2026)

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval
and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month (21 days per year). Up to 5 unused days
carry over into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited on January 31.

## Equipment
New hires receive a laptop budget of $2,400 and a one-time home-office stipend of $600.
Equipment must be returned within 14 days of leaving the company.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Hotel spend is capped at $250 per night,
or $350 per night in New York, San Francisco and London.

## Product: the Kestrel arm
The Kestrel K2 robotic arm has a payload of 7.5 kg, a reach of 920 mm and repeatability of
±0.03 mm. It replaced the K1 (payload 5 kg) in March 2025.
